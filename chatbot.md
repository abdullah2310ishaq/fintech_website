Meet Our Founder

Founded by Adeel Moghal, a seasoned finance professional with over two decades of global banking experience, FinCan bridges the gap between entrepreneurs and lenders.

Mergers & Acquisitions Advisory
We provide mergers and acquisitions advisory services for businesses seeking growth, expansion, investment, or successful transition opportunities. Our approach focuses on helping clients assess potential transactions, understand business value, review strategic fit, and structure opportunities in a professional and practical manner.

From acquisition planning and business sale preparation to merger support and strategic partnership advisory, we help businesses move through complex transactions with clarity, confidence, and a focus on long-term value.
Financing & Business Planning
We provide financing and business planning services for businesses seeking capital, expansion support, project funding, or structured financial planning. Our services are designed to support a wide range of business needs, from startup financing to large-scale commercial, real estate, equipment, and infrastructure financing.

We assist clients in preparing strong business plans, understanding financing requirements, structuring funding opportunities, and presenting their projects professionally to potential lenders, investors, and financial partners.

Our Financing & Business Planning Services Include:

Startup Financing :
We support startups and growing businesses in preparing for financing opportunities ranging from $100,000 to $50 million, depending on the project size, business model, and funding requirements.

Real Estate Financing :
We assist clients with financing solutions for real estate projects, including property development, acquisition, investment, and commercial real estate opportunities.

Commercial Financing :
We provide support for businesses seeking commercial financing for expansion, operations, acquisitions, working capital, or other business growth needs.

Equipment Financing :
We help businesses explore financing options for purchasing or upgrading essential equipment, machinery, vehicles, or operational assets.

Infrastructure Financing :
We support larger projects that require structured financing for infrastructure development, construction, expansion, or long-term investment.

Business Planning :
We prepare professional business plans that clearly present the business model, financial projections, market opportunity, growth strategy, and funding requirements.

Financial Consulting
We provide strategic financial consulting services to support businesses at every stage of growth. Our approach focuses on understanding your business goals, reviewing your financial position, and developing practical strategies that improve decision-making, cash flow, profitability, and long-term financial stability.

From financial planning and business performance analysis to funding preparation and growth strategy, we help you build a stronger financial foundation and move forward with confidence.

Business Management Consulting
We provide strategic business management consulting services designed to help organizations operate more efficiently, manage growth, and make better business decisions. Our consulting approach focuses on understanding your current business structure, identifying areas for improvement, and developing practical strategies that support performance, productivity, and long-term success.

From operational improvement and business planning to growth strategy and management support, we help businesses build stronger foundations and create clear pathways for future development.
