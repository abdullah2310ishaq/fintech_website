Graphics and Visual Direction
Overall direction:
The graphics should support the message that FinCan helps business owners prepare and structure financing requests. Avoid visuals that suggest insurance, retirement planning, stock trading, wealth management, luxury lifestyle, or generic corporate finance.
Recommended hero graphic:
A professional business owner and advisor reviewing financial documents, projections, or a lender package. Another good option is a clean dashboard-style graphic showing Business Plan, Financial Projections, Loan Proposal, and Lender Review.
Recommended service icons:
•	Business Plans: document icon
•	Financial Projections: chart or forecast icon
•	Loan Proposals: bank/document icon
•	Refinancing: circular arrows with dollar sign icon
•	Business Acquisition: storefront or transaction icon
•	Real Estate and Construction: building, blueprint, or crane icon
•	Management Consulting: strategy or checklist icon
Recommended process graphic:
Use a simple four-step horizontal process: Understand the Transaction, Review the Numbers, Build the Package, Support the Presentation.
Images to avoid:
•	Families with retirement themes
•	Insurance-related graphics
•	Stock market trading screens
•	Luxury lifestyle images
•	Gold coins or wealth-building visuals
•	Generic handshake images that do not explain the service
Tone and Design Notes for Developer
•	Use clear business language. Avoid vague phrases such as financial journey, lifelong security, wealth-building, or retirement planning.
•	Keep the navigation simple: Home, Services, Process, About, Contact.
•	Use action-focused headings that explain the benefit to the visitor.
•	Use short sections with enough white space so the website is easy to scan.
•	Place a Book Your Free Consultation button in the hero section, service section, and footer.
•	Use consistent terminology: financial consulting, management consulting, business plans, projections, loan proposals, lender-ready packages.
•	Do not say lender-approved or guaranteed financing. Use lender-ready, prepared for lender review, or professionally structured.
